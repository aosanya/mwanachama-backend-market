# Requirements

The module serves any agency where people post what they sell or pay and others pick among the posts. Requirements:

- A poster posts an offer: a price, and bulk prices, in his own words for the product.
- A person who wants something sees every poster's offer and picks one, or asks the posters she chooses, or puts up a notice.
- A buy is either alone, asked of the one poster she picked, or in a group order that a coordinator starts from a posted bulk price and sends when it is ready.
- Every step that commits someone waits for that person's own yes. The AI asks, tells, drafts and records, and never says yes for a person.
- Nothing runs on a timer: a person who does not answer simply has no answer yet.
- An order keeps the terms and the workflow version it started with, and every order has a trail of who did what.
- Each person sees only what the design allows.
- An agency's draft can be tested in a sandbox, scenario by scenario, and the trail shows what transpired.
